What we do with the details you send us

This page covers this website only. How your business records are handled is set out in the licence you sign, which you see in full before you agree to anything.

What we collect here

This website collects nothing by itself. The call request form writes an email in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you press send. The fit check on the home page runs in your browser and your answers are not stored or sent.

What we receive

If you send the email, we receive what you wrote: your name, company, email, country, and a phone number or note if you added one.

Why we use it

To reply to you and arrange a call. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share these details.

How long we keep it

If we do not end up working together, we delete your email within 12 months.

Where the site is hosted

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security.

Your choices

Email hello@tremolv.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.
