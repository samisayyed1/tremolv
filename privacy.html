What we do with the details you send us

This page covers this website only. How your business records are handled is set out in the licence you sign, which you see in full before you agree to anything.

What we collect here

Only what you type into the call request form: your name, company, email, country, and a phone number or note if you add one. The fit check on the home page runs in your browser. Your answers are not stored or sent, apart from the one-line result, which is attached to your request if you send one.

Why we collect it

To reply to you and arrange a call. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share these details.

Where it is kept

Form submissions are stored by our website host, Netlify, where only we can read them.

How long we keep it

If we do not end up working together, we delete your request within 12 months.

Your choices

Email hello@tremolv.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.
