License the how. Keep the who.

Every business holds two kinds of information. One is about people. The other is about how the work gets done. Only the second kind is worth anything to an AI developer, and only the second kind ever leaves.

The who

Removed before a file is shown to anyone.

  • Customer and staff names
  • Phone numbers and email addresses

  • Account, card and payment details
  • Order and ticket numbers that could trace back

  • How an order moves from enquiry to delivery

  • The words your customers really use

    Questions owners ask us.

    So you sell my data to big AI companies?

    We license a de-identified copy of your operational records to AI developers. Before you sign, you see which company, which file and what you are paid. You can say no to any of them.

    How much will I be paid?

    We cannot tell you until a buyer makes a written offer, and we will not guess. What moves the figure is how many years of history you have, how much of it is real conversation, and how cleanly you own it.

    You see the offer before you decide. Your share is 60% of it.

    What if my customers find out?

    There is nothing about your customers in the file, and you can read it yourself before it goes. If you have promised a customer in writing that you will not share their records, those records stay out of scope. We ask about this on the first call.

    Why would anyone pay for my ordinary records?

    AI systems have read the public internet. What they have not seen is how real work gets done: a complaint from first message to resolution, an order from enquiry to delivery. Your records hold that, in the words your customers really use.

    What will you not take?

    Patient records. Customer records from banks, lenders and insurers. Anything about children. Records you hold on behalf of your own clients, because those belong to them.

    Is this legal?

    The licence is a written contract you sign, and personal details are removed first. Privacy law differs by country, so each country has its own annex to the licence. Take it to your accountant or your lawyer before you sign.

    Which countries do you work in?

    Any country where we can meet the local privacy law. We started in India.

    You are new. Why should I trust you?

    We are new, and you would be among our first ten partners. That is why you see every file, name every buyer you refuse, and walk away at no cost if nothing sells.
